# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Industrial plant managers, maintenance engineers, procurement specialists, and internal Reethau site administrators managing clean energy systems and spare part inventories across operational sites (Cikarang, Karawang, Semarang, Surabaya).

## Product Purpose

Reethau Clean Energy Inventory Management App serves a dual purpose: presenting PT Reethau Clean Energy's sustainable industrial energy supply chain (CNG, LNG, Biomass) to stakeholders while providing an internal inventory portal for tracking and transferring critical spare parts across operational sites.

## Positioning

The premier industrial clean energy showcase and multi-site inventory intelligence platform for high-reliability energy operations in Indonesia.

## Brand Personality

Technical authority, sustainable innovation, operational clarity, expert confidence, dark-mode sleekness.

## Anti-references

Over-saturated generic SaaS dashboards, washed-out low-contrast gray text on dark cards, generic stock layouts with uninspired icons, hyper-rounded (32px+) bloated cards, decorative gradient text hacks.

## Design Principles

- High Contrast Precision: Absolute legibility across dark surfaces with OKLCH-derived neon accents and high contrast text (>= 4.5:1).
- Purposeful Motion: Eased-out, high-performance transitions and micro-interactions that feel responsive without unnecessary bounce or layout layout shifts.
- Information Density with Breathing Room: Dense technical telemetry and spare part data balanced with deliberate vertical rhythm and clean spatial hierarchy.
- Seamless Dual Register: Smooth progression between public-facing clean energy story and internal high-productivity inventory dashboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Targeting WCAG 2.1 AA compliance with high-contrast text ratios, clear focus rings for keyboard navigation, screen-reader aria labels on interactive elements, and prefers-reduced-motion fallback support.
